QUESTION:
What is the attendance requirement to sit in the Major exam?

GENERATED RESPONSE:
According to IIT Jodhpur Academic Regulations (Section 4.1.9 & Circular No. IITJ/OAA/Circular/2024-25/51682):

• **Minimum Attendance Requirement**: A minimum of **75% attendance** is mandatory in each course (covering lectures, tutorials, and practical sessions) to be eligible to sit for the Major Examination.
• **Condonation (65% to 75%)**: Condonation may be considered for attendance between 65% and 75% on genuine medical grounds, subject to a certificate issued/endorsed by the Institute Medical Officer (IMO) and written approval of the course instructor.
• **Below 65% (DX Grade)**: Attendance below 65% attracts an automatic **DX (Debarred)** grade, and the student will NOT be permitted to appear in the Major Examination.

QUESTION:
What happens if my attendance in a course falls below 65%?

GENERATED RESPONSE:
According to IIT Jodhpur Academic Regulations (Section 4.1.9 & Circular No. IITJ/OAA/Circular/2024-25/51682):

• **Attendance Strictly Below 65%**: If a student's attendance in a course falls below 65%, they are awarded an automatic **DX (Debarred)** grade for that course.
• **Disqualification**: A student awarded a DX grade is strictly debarred and disqualified from appearing in the Major Examination.
• **Remediation**: The student must re-register for the course in a subsequent semester to earn the credits.

QUESTION:
What are the rules for make-up examinations if I miss Minor 1 due to illness?

GENERATED RESPONSE:
According to IIT Jodhpur Make-up Examination Policies (Section 8.4 & Circular No. IITJ/OAA/Circular/2024-25/51682):

• **Application Timeline**: The student must submit a formal application for a make-up examination within **3 working days** of missing the examination (extended up to 10 days only in cases of documented hospitalization).
• **Medical Endorsement**: The application must be supported by medical documents endorsed by the Institute Medical Officer (IMO).
• **Minor Exam Makeup**: Requires written permission and scheduling by the respective Course Instructor.
• **Prohibition**: Prorating of examination marks or attendance is strictly prohibited under institute regulations.

QUESTION:
Can I withdraw from a course after the add-drop deadline, and what grade is awarded?

GENERATED RESPONSE:
According to IIT Jodhpur Course Registration and Withdrawal Rules (Section 5.3):

• **Withdrawal Deadline**: Course withdrawal is permitted up to four weeks after semester commencement (or up to 2 weeks after Minor 1 examinations), subject to faculty advisor approval.
• **Transcript Notation**: The course will be awarded a **W grade** on the student grade card, indicating withdrawal without penalizing the SGPA/CGPA.
• **Minimum Credit Limit**: The student's registered credits after withdrawal must not drop below the minimum required semester load (12 credits).
